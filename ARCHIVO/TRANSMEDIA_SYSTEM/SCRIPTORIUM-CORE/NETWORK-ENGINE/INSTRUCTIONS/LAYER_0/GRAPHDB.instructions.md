What is GraphDB?[](https://graphdb.ontotext.com/documentation/11.3/index.html#what-is-graphdb "Permalink to this headline")

Good places to start for...

-   [Everyone](https://graphdb.ontotext.com/documentation/11.3/userroles.html#role-everyone): All users, especially new ones, who can benefit from introductions to basic GraphDB capabilities.

-   [Knowledge engineers](https://graphdb.ontotext.com/documentation/11.3/userroles.html#role-knowledge-engineer): People who focus on the technologies and standards for creating and taking advantage of knowledge graphs.

-   [Data scientists](https://graphdb.ontotext.com/documentation/11.3/userroles.html#role-data-scientist): People who use a variety of data formats and tools, often in different combinations, to identify patterns that help to answer questions.

-   [Developers](https://graphdb.ontotext.com/documentation/11.3/userroles.html#role-developer): People who are building an application that uses GraphDB as a backend.

-   [Administrators](https://graphdb.ontotext.com/documentation/11.3/userroles.html#role-admin): People who are installing and maintaining a GraphDB system or an application that uses GraphDB as the backend.

Ontotext GraphDB is a highly efficient, scalable and robust [graph database](https://graphdb.ontotext.com/documentation/11.3/glossary.html#glossary-sr) with [RDF](https://graphdb.ontotext.com/documentation/11.3/introduction-to-semantic-web.html#introduction-to-semantic-web-rdf) and [SPARQL](https://graphdb.ontotext.com/documentation/11.3/introduction-to-semantic-web.html#introduction-to-semantic-web-sparql) support. GraphDB is one of the few triplestores that can perform real-time [semantic inferencing](https://graphdb.ontotext.com/documentation/11.3/introduction-to-semantic-web.html#introduction-to-semantic-web-reasoning-strategies) at scale, allowing users to derive new semantic facts from existing facts.

With excellent enterprise features, integration with external search applications, LLM Graph Retrieval-Augmented Generation (Graph RAG) capabilities, and compatibility with industry standards, GraphDB is the preferred database choice of both small independent developers and big enterprises.

Highlights of GraphDB include:

-   Dedicated community and commercial support.

-   Integration with Elasticsearch, OpenSearch, Lucene, Solr, and other high-performance search applications.

-   Clusters and multi-region deployments support.

-   [Connectors](https://graphdb.ontotext.com/documentation/11.3/connectors.html), which provide fast keyword and faceted searches that are typically implemented by an external component. All GraphDB editions include connectors for Lucene and ChatGPT, and the Enterprise Edition includes connectors for Solr, Elasticsearch, OpenSearch, and Kafka.

-   Implementation of the [RDF4J](https://rdf4j.org/about/) framework interfaces and the [W3C SPARQL Protocol specification](https://www.w3.org/TR/sparql11-overview/). Learn more about RDF4J and the roles of its layers in the [Architecture and components](https://graphdb.ontotext.com/documentation/11.3/architecture-components.html) chapter of this documentation's Reference section.

-   Support for all RDF serialization formats.

-   Availability as a [cloud deployment](https://graphdb.ontotext.com/documentation/11.3/cloud-deployment.html) on [Amazon Web Services](https://graphdb.ontotext.com/documentation/11.3/aws-deployment.html) and on [Microsoft Azure](https://graphdb.ontotext.com/documentation/11.3/azure-deployment.html).

Reasoning and query evaluation are performed over a persistent storage layer. Loading, [reasoning](https://graphdb.ontotext.com/documentation/11.3/reasoning.html), and query evaluation proceed extremely quickly even against huge [ontologies](https://graphdb.ontotext.com/documentation/11.3/glossary.html#glossary-ont) and [knowledge bases](https://graphdb.ontotext.com/documentation/11.3/glossary.html#glossary-kb).

GraphDB can manage billions of explicit statements on desktop hardware and handle tens of billions of statements on commodity server hardware. According to the [LDBC Semantic Publishing Benchmark](https://ldbcouncil.org/developer/spb), it is one of the most scalable OWL repositories currently available.

Ontotext [offers licenses for two editions of GraphDB:](https://www.ontotext.com/products/graphdb/download/)

-   GraphDB Free

-   GraphDB Enterprise (EE)

The GraphDB Standard Edition (SE), while no longer offered, still receives legacy support.
